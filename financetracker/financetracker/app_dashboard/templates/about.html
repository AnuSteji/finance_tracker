About Personal Finance Tracker

System Introduction

The Personal Finance Tracker is a web-based application designed to help users manage their financial activities efficiently. The system enables users to record their income, monitor expenses, and set monthly budgets.

With the increasing need for financial planning, this system provides a simple and effective way for individuals, students, and small businesses to track their financial transactions and maintain better control over their spending.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an easy-to-use financial tracking platform that helps users understand their financial habits, reduce unnecessary spending, and improve savings.

Our Vision

Our vision is to empower individuals and businesses with digital tools that simplify financial management and promote smarter financial decisions.

Main Features of the System

Income Management

Add and track multiple income sources easily.

Expense Tracking

Monitor daily expenses and categorize spending.

Budget Management

Set monthly budgets and manage spending limits.

Over Budget Detection

Get alerts when your spending exceeds the set budget.

Email Notification

Receive notifications about budget alerts and updates.

Loan Eligibility Prediction

Predict loan eligibility based on income and expenses.
